RocketLauncher

A clean, fast, free, open-source launcher made to keep your Apps, Games, Folders and even Websites exactly where you want them.



Download Now Patch Notes

Features

  • Launching games from any other launcher or standalone .exe files.
  • Simple drag and drop to add a game.
  • Automatically fetches a game banner when a game is added via drag and drop.
  • The ability to add and launch web links in the default browser.
  • The ability to add folders and use them as shortcuts.
  • Adding custom arguments to standalone exe apps.
  • Shell-mode launching for apps needing separate terminals (like emulators).
  • Adding Microsoft Store/Xbox games to the app and launching them from it.
  • Remotely launch apps from other devices on your local network.
  • Controller navigation.
  • Filtering apps into categories that can be shown whenever needed.
  • Multi select mode for easy app management

The Vision

RocketLauncher aims to be the go-to launcher for anyone who wants a simple, convenient way to contain their apps and games. No unnecessary accounts. No random extra junk. Just a snappy, FOSS launcher that gives you the features you actually need without wasting your time.

The Story

RocketLauncher began as a simple fix for a very messy desktop. The options weren't great: leave them cluttering the screen, hide everything and dig through folders every time you want to play something, rely on third-party launchers that usually miss the mark, or build a new launcher from scratch.

So the fourth option won. It took more than a year of making, but the result was worth it. A fast, feature-rich launcher that finally puts everything where it belongs.

My hope is: that you, dear visitor, get the same usefulness out of it that I did.
