Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1 October 2025, 06:08 GMT

This Privacy Policy explains how Entry Bot handles your data.

1. Data Collected

  • When you use the register command, Entry Bot collects:
    • Your Discord User ID
    • The audio file you upload
  • No other personal information is collected.

2. How Data is Used

  • Your User ID is used to associate you with your uploaded intro.
  • The audio file is stored so Entry Bot can play it when you join a voice channel.

3. Data Sharing

  • Your data is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Data is only used within Entry Bot’s functionality.

4. Data Removal

  • You can delete your data at any time by using the unregister command. This will remove both your User ID and your audio file from the Bot’s storage.

5. Data Retention

  • Data is stored until you remove it with the unregister command or until Entry Bot is shut down.

6. Children’s Privacy

  • Entry Bot is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, you may not use the Bot.

  • The Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting.
