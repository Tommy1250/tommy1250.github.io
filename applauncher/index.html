App Launcher

A Fully featured launcher for all your favourite apps or games (And even websites)

Features

My app launcher has alot of features like:

1. Launching games from any other launcher or standalone .exe files.
2. Simple drag and drop to add a game.
3. Automatically fetches a game banner when a game is added via drag and drop
4. The ability to add and launch web links in the default browser.
5. The ability to add folders and use them as shortcuts.
6. Adding custom arguments to standalone exe apps.
7. Shell-mode launching for apps needing separate terminals (like emulators).
8. Adding microsoft store/xbox games to the app and launching them from it.
9. Remotely launch apps from other devices on your local network.
10. Controller navigation.
11. Filtering apps into categories that can be shown whenever needed

The vision

I want AppLauncher to be the go-to launcher for anyone who wants a simple convinient way to contain their apps and games
No Unneccesary accounts.
No Extra stuff for no reason.
Snappy FOSS launcher with all the features.

The Applauncher started as a simple desktop icons handler because i wanted to contain my desktop icons somewhere other than the desktop.
So i had one of these solutions:
1. Leave a 100+ icons on the desktop obstructing my wallpaper.
2. Hide the icons and launch the apps by navigating to their respective folders or launch them from their launchers.
3. Use a 3rd party launcher whitch don't always have all the feature.
4. Make my owh launcher with all the features i need in a launcher.

I opted for the 4th option although it was really hard.
spent more than a year making the launcher just to store my desktop icons somewhere.
Overall it was worth it. the app is feature rich and very snappy.
I Hope You Dear Reader would find it useful as i have.
